# Ember Pact — Duel of the Four Winds

An original duel card game for the [make.fun best card game bounty](https://make.fun/competitions/best-card-game-20261122-1826). Bring the Hollow Warden's life from 30 to 0 before yours falls.

Four invented suits — Ember, Tide, Thorn, Gale — each with its own colour, shape and pact bonus. Play two cards of one suit in a turn and its pact fires: Ember burns the rival for 2, Tide draws a card, Thorn grows 4 shield, Gale grants 2 energy. Creatures carry Guard (must be attacked first), Swift (strikes the moment it is played) and Drain (heals you by the damage it deals).

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. Keyboard: 1–7 plays a card from your hand, Space ends your turn, R starts a new duel.
